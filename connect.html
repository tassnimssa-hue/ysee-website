A Little Beginning

This website means more to me than just being a project. It is my first step into web development.

The idea of YSEE originally came from yousra. As for me, I had always wanted to try building a website, but I was honestly afraid to start. I kept thinking that I wasn’t ready, that I didn’t know enough, and I kept postponing it every day.

But Basra helped me overcome that fear. She encouraged me, supported me, and gave me the push I needed to finally say: “Okay, let’s try.” 🤍

I started learning, experimenting, making mistakes, fixing them, and slowly discovering that programming wasn’t something I needed to be afraid of.

I’m really grateful to her for believing in me and for helping me take this first step. Thank you, my dear yousra. 🫶🏻

This website may not be perfect, and we still have so much to learn and so many things to improve, but that’s what makes it special to me. It reminds me that sometimes the hardest part is simply starting.

This is only the beginning. There are still so many ideas, projects, challenges, and beautiful things waiting for us ahead. 🌱

I’m happy that I finally stopped postponing and gave myself the chance to try.

Our first website.
Our first step.
And definitely not our last. 🤍✨
